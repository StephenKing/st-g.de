In October, I gave a talk at SREcon EMEA, titled `Monitoring a Telco`, where I explained how I got involved in using Apache Flink for monitoring [emnify's](https://www.emnify.com) telco network by processing packet captures.

More information can be found on the [USENIX conference page](https://www.usenix.org/conference/srecon26emea/presentation/gebert), the recording on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mZW8vipp0Cw). Enjoy watching!
